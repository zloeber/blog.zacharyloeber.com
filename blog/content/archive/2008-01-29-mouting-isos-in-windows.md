I just downloaded the 4 iso images needed to install vista and wanted to use [vlite][1] to make the 4 iso&#8217;s into one while at the same time removing a bunch of extra crap that gets installed by default. Well I have no desire to burn 4 discs just to have to copy them all back to the hard drive so I looked for an iso mounter to virtually access the files I needed. [MagicISO  Virtual CD/DVD-ROM][2] (freeware) fit the bill perfectly.
