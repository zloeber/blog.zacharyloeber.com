# Understanding AI

## An AI course for your kids

---

# What is AI?

Computer systems that can perform tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as:

- Learning
- Problem-solving
- Reasoning
- Perception

---

# Large Language Models (LLMs)

Recent breakthroughs in LLMs have revolutionized AI capabilities:

- Process and understand human language
- Generate human-like text
- Learn from vast amounts of data

> **QUESTION** Where can we get vast amounts of data?

---

# Impact of LLMs

How LLMs Have Shifted the AI Landscape

LLMs have enabled:

- Improved chatbots and virtual assistants
- Enhanced language translation
- Advanced text summarization and generation

---

# Uses of AI

AI in Our Daily Lives

AI is used in:

- Virtual assistants (e.g., Siri, Alexa)
- Image recognition (e.g., Facebook, Google Photos)
- Personalized recommendations (e.g., Netflix, Amazon)
- Language translation (e.g., Google Translate)

---

# Chat-Based AI

Getting the Most out of Chat-Based AI

Tips for effective interactions:

- Be specific and clear with your prompts
- Use natural language and avoid jargon
- Ask follow-up questions to refine results

---

# Prompt Engineering

Basic Prompt Engineering Techniques

- Use quotes for exact phrases
- Define context with keywords
- Specify desired output format
